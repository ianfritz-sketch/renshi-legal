# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 4, 2026
**Last Updated:** May 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Renshi** ("we," "our," or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Renshi mobile application.

Renshi is operated by Ian Fritz as a sole proprietorship based in Utah, United States. Contact: renshicombat@yahoo.com.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you create an account and use Renshi, we collect:

**Account information.** Email address and password (passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text), display name, username (handle), self-selected training track (e.g., wrestling, BJJ, boxing, MMA), and an optional bio.

**Profile content.** Profile photo and banner image, if you choose to upload them.

**Workout data.** Activity type, intensity, duration, notes, focus areas (e.g., muscle groups, drilling focus), conditioning type, recovery type, and an optional photo attached to the workout.

**Social data.** Follow relationships (who you follow and who follows you), team memberships, posts you share to the public Feed, and badges you've unlocked.

**Subscription data.** Subscription status, plan (monthly or yearly), trial dates, and renewal status. Payment information (card details) is handled entirely by Apple and RevenueCat — we never see or store your payment method.

**Device and usage data.** Basic technical information necessary to operate the app, such as session tokens and timestamps.

We do not collect location data, contacts, or any biometric information.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to operate, maintain, and improve Renshi. Specifically:

- To authenticate your account and keep your data synced across devices
- To compute leaderboards, streaks, XP, and badges based on the workouts you log
- To display your public profile, posts, and team memberships to other users where you've chosen to make them visible
- To process subscriptions and manage trial periods
- To respond to support requests
- To detect and prevent abuse

We do **not** sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for cross-platform tracking.

## 3. How Your Information Is Shared

Your information is shared only with the following service providers, who process data on our behalf:

- **Supabase** — hosts our database, authentication system, and file storage (profile photos, workout photos). [Supabase Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy)
- **RevenueCat** — processes subscription state and entitlement validation. [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)
- **Apple** — processes payments, manages In-App Purchases, and distributes the app via the App Store. [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- **Expo** — provides build infrastructure; receives no end-user data.

Other Renshi users can see information you've made public: your display name, handle, profile photo, banner, bio, training track, badges, leaderboard ranking, team membership, posts you share to the Feed, and aggregate workout statistics (e.g., XP, streaks, time-in-category). Specific workout details (notes, intensity, photos) are visible only to you unless you explicitly share them to the Feed.

We will disclose your information if required by law, valid legal process, or to protect the safety, rights, or property of users or the public.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from **Settings → Delete Account**, or by emailing us at renshicombat@yahoo.com. Deletion is permanent and removes your profile, workouts, follows, posts, and badges from our database within 30 days.

## 5. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Object to or restrict certain processing
- Receive a portable copy of your data

To exercise any of these rights, email renshicombat@yahoo.com. We respond within 30 days.

## 6. Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), encrypted password storage, and Supabase's row-level security to ensure each user can only access their own data. No system is perfect, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Renshi is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. International Users

Renshi is operated from the United States. If you use the App from outside the U.S., your data will be transferred to and processed in the U.S., where data protection laws may differ from your country.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the "Last Updated" date above and, for material changes, notify users in the App or by email. Continued use of Renshi after a change means you accept the updated policy.

## 10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email **renshicombat@yahoo.com**.
