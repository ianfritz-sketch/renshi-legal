Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple, not Renshi. On your iPhone, open Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions, tap Renshi, then tap Cancel Subscription. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds for App Store subscriptions are issued by Apple, not Renshi. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the Renshi charge, and tap Report a Problem.

How do the free trial and billing work?

Every new account gets a single 7-day free trial. After the trial, continued access requires a paid subscription:

  • Monthly: $2.99 per month, auto-renewing
  • Yearly: $29.99 per year, auto-renewing (saves ~16% vs monthly)

Apple charges the renewal to your Apple ID within 24 hours of the period ending. Cancel anytime in your iPhone Settings.

I can't log in / my account isn't working

Try resetting your password from the sign-in screen. If that doesn't help, email us at renshicombat@yahoo.com with the email address on your account and a brief description of what's happening.

How do I delete my account?

Open Renshi → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, workouts, photos, and all associated data. Subscriptions are managed separately through Apple — cancel those in your iPhone Settings before deleting.

How do I report another user, a post, or content I think is harmful?

Every feed post, profile, and team has a ⋯ menu with a Report option. For minor-safety concerns, use the dedicated Minor in danger report option or email renshicombat@yahoo.com with the subject "Minor safety report". We treat those as the highest priority.

How do I report a bug?

Email renshicombat@yahoo.com with:

  • What you were doing when it happened
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • A screenshot or screen recording if you can

How do I submit a copyright infringement (DMCA) notice?

See the Copyright Policy (DMCA) section of our Terms of Service for the full procedure, required information, and our Designated Agent contact.

Compatibility

Renshi requires iPhone running iOS 16 or later. iPad and Android are not supported at this time.
